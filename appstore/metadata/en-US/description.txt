simple-capture records your screen, your camera and your voice, lets you tidy up the take in a light editor, and exports a file that is ready to send. There is no account, and simple-capture makes no network connections: what you record stays on your Mac.

RECORD
• A display, a window, every window of one app, a region you draw, or only your camera.
• An iPhone or iPad connected by cable, with its screen and its sound.
• An optional camera overlay: circle, square, rectangle or rounded rectangle, with mirror, border and shadow. Place, size and zoom it in the live Preview window, which shows exactly what the video will look like.
• Your microphone and system audio: everything you hear, or only the app you are recording. simple-capture never records its own sounds.
• Frame shapes for anywhere you post: original, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, with a solid, gradient or image background, and Fit or Fill.
• 30 or 60 fps at Retina or 1× resolution, the cursor with optional click highlights, and a countdown.
• Switch to another source while recording, pause and resume, or restart the take. A small control bar, the menu bar and global keyboard shortcuts keep the controls at hand, and none of simple-capture's own windows appear in the video.
• Every source is saved to its own crash-safe track. If the app is force-quit or crashes, you lose about a second at most, and the next launch recovers the recording.

EDIT
• Trim, split, cut, copy, paste and reorder clips; change speed from 0.5× to 4× with the pitch kept; undo and redo; autosave.
• J/K/L shuttle, frame stepping and snapping.
• Separate screen and camera tracks, camera placement keyframes, per-track volume and mute with waveforms, and silence removal.
• Blur or black out anything you don't want to show.
• Edits never change the original recording.

TEXT
• Text on your video in 13 bundled font families or the system fonts, with color emoji, outlines, shadows, glow, background boxes, gradients and layer order.
• In, out and loop animations (fade, pop, slide, typewriter, word by word, bounce, pulse, wiggle and float) that look the same at every frame rate.

EXPORT
• MP4 with H.264 or HEVC, encoded by Apple's VideoToolbox, or AV1.
• MOV with ProRes 422, H.264 or HEVC, for editing in other apps.
• WebM with VP9, VP8 or AV1.
• GIF with an optimized palette, dithering and loop settings, and animated WebP.
• Quality presets, constant quality or bitrate, or a file-size limit, with size and time estimates before you start.
• Ready-made settings for email, team chat, video sites and vertical short-form video; a frame rate remembered for each format; resizing with the aspect ratio locked; and your own named presets.
• Save next to the recording or in a folder you choose, then Show in Finder, Copy or Open.

LIBRARY
• Every recording in one place with a poster frame, date and size. Search, rename, edit, export, show in Finder or move to the Trash.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics, and the app makes no network connections.
• Waveforms, silence detection and encoding all happen on your Mac.
• Recordings are saved in Movies/simple-capture on your Mac.
• macOS asks for Screen Recording, Camera and Microphone access only when you press Allow, and only for what you use.

simple-capture is open source under the MIT license. It requires macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
