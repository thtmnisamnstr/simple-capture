simple-capture is a screen recorder with a small video editor built in. Record a display, a window, an app, or a region you draw, with your camera in a corner and your mic and system audio on their own tracks. An iPhone or iPad screen and camera work as input sources too, both wired and wireless. Before you record, pick a frame shape to match where the video is going, from 16:9 down to vertical 9:16 for short-form video.

When you stop, the editor opens. Trim and split clips, speed them up, blur anything private, and add text with outlines, shadows, and simple animations.

Export to MP4, MOV, WebM, GIF, or animated WebP. There are presets for email, team chat, video sites, and vertical video, and you get a size estimate before the export starts.

You don't need an account, and the app never connects to the internet. Recordings stay on your Mac.

Open source under the MIT license. Requires macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
